## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by the methodology seat): From an editorial-oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the study design, the way data are collected, filtered and split, the reporting of statistics and uncertainty, and the materials offered for replication would hold up under expert refereeing in this subfield. It is the gating dimension of the contract. A fatal finding from any reviewer routes the submission to rejection. A block or a warn from any reviewer routes it to major revision. The methodology seat is the only role eligible to score it, so I make no scoring plan for it. My oversight duty is to make sure the panel's handling of D1 follows the contract's decision logic.

D2 (writing_and_structure, normal priority, owned by the Journal-Fit seat): This dimension covers how the manuscript is organised, how clearly it explains its contribution and procedure, and whether it follows the presentation norms that readers of method-centred work at leading vision and learning venues expect. It is the only dimension I score. Under the contract, a warn or a block from any reviewer on this dimension yields minor revision, unless a more severe D1 trigger takes precedence. Acceptance requires every dimension to pass for every reviewer. Because the dimension is non-mandatory, it has no fatal tier.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: An opening that names the problem, the gap in current practice and an explicit list of contributions, each of which the body later delivers; a method exposition ordered so a competent reader could re-implement it, with each symbol defined once and used consistently and with any pipeline diagram matching the prose; related work that places the contribution against prior measurement approaches instead of only listing them; experiments grouped by the question each answers, with self-contained captioned figures and tables cited from the text; precise and consistent terminology for perceived appearance or demographic attributes; explicit limitations plus a responsible-use or ethics discussion, as expected for fairness-oriented vision work; a clean split between essential main-text material and supplementary detail; and section lengths proportionate to their importance, without padding or repetition.
what_triggers_block: Organisational or expository defects that stop a reader from following or reproducing the core contribution: contributions are missing, or are promised up front and never delivered; the core procedure is presented out of order or relies on undefined or conflicting notation, so a key step cannot be reconstructed; headline results appear only in uncited, uncaptioned or text-contradicting figures or tables; limitations and responsible-use discussion are absent for a fairness-related measurement contribution; or material needed to understand the main claim exists only in supplementary files.
what_triggers_warn: The argument can be followed end to end, but the exposition is uneven: some sections are verbose or repetitive; related work catalogues instead of positioning; terminology or notation is inconsistent yet recoverable from context; captions need the main text to be understood; there are minor departures from venue formatting or section conventions; limitations are present but thin; or the length balance is off, for example background that dwarfs the method and evaluation sections.
