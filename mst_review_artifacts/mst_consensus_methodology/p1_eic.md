## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor; mandatory; owned by the methodology seat). From an editorial-oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the way the study was designed, how its data were collected and processed, how its statistics are reported, and what it gives others to reproduce it would all hold up under a competent referee in this field. For a human-annotation or measurement study in ML fairness, that bar covers the annotation protocol, how raters were recruited and who they are, how stimuli were sampled, which agreement or subjectivity measures were chosen and why, how uncertainty is quantified, and whether instructions, raw per-rater labels, and analysis code are released. It is the decisive dimension of this contract: a fatal block from any reviewer ends in rejection, and a block or a warn from any reviewer forces at least a major revision. Scoring it is the methodology reviewer's job, not mine; I record it here only so that my own scoring stays in its lane and neither duplicates nor pre-empts that judgement.

D2 (writing_and_structure; normal priority; owned by the eic seat). This dimension asks whether a reader can follow the manuscript from question to protocol to findings to implications, whether the exposition is clear and its terminology stable, and whether it meets the conventions readers expect of a method-centred submission: a clear contributions statement, methods that can be located and followed, an honest limitations and ethics discussion, and the documentation a datasets-and-benchmarks style venue normally requires. This is the dimension I score. A warn or worse from any reviewer pulls the outcome down to minor revision on this dimension alone, and acceptance requires every dimension, this one included, to pass.

## Scoring Plan

### D2: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D2
what_to_look_for: A contributions statement that names the measurement question and the specific claims it supports; a section order running from motivation to annotation protocol to analysis to implications with no reliance on undefined constructs; one consistent, defined vocabulary for the measured construct, the rating scale, and each agreement or subjectivity statistic; figures and tables that stand alone with labelled axes, units, sample sizes, and uncertainty; a methods section from which a reader can locate the annotator pool, instructions, interface, stimulus set, and analysis plan without hunting through appendices; a substantive limitations and ethical-considerations section for work involving human raters and perceived sensitive attributes; and the venue-expected documentation (datasheet- or data-card-style description, licence and access statement, pointers to supplementary material).
what_triggers_block: Structural failures that stop a reader from reconstructing or evaluating the work: no identifiable contributions statement or research questions; the annotation protocol, rater pool, or analysis pipeline cannot be found or is scattered piecemeal across sections; the core construct or scale is never defined or shifts meaning so that headline findings become ambiguous; headline claims in the abstract or conclusion have no matching results section; limitations or ethics discussion is absent despite human annotators and sensitive attributes; or required venue documentation is missing to the extent that sections must be rewritten rather than line-edited.
what_triggers_warn: Localised clarity defects a careful reader can work around: a few inconsistent or undefined uses of construct, scale, or statistic names; key protocol details moved to appendices without in-text pointers; figures or tables missing sample sizes or uncertainty; a limitations section that exists but is perfunctory; abstract or introduction claims worded more strongly than the results sections support; or gaps in venue-expected documentation that can be filled without restructuring.
